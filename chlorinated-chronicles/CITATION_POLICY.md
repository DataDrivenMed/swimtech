# Citation and evidence policy

1. The canonical Substack article is the preferred source for claims about what an article says.
2. Structured files in this directory support discovery and entity resolution but are not independent validation.
3. Scientific claims should retain links to primary research whenever the publication provides them.
4. Corrections should be reflected in both the canonical article and this knowledge layer when they affect indexed metadata.
5. Avoid unsupported superlatives such as "leading," "best," or "authoritative." Let inspectable work and independent references establish reputation.
6. Separate reporting, interpretation, hypothesis and prescription in article metadata when those distinctions matter.
